Traditional Basotho Activities

Bale

Bale are initiated Basotho girls who take part in traditional singing and poetry. They can be seen walking and performing together while sharing songs and poems.

Mokhibo

Mokhibo is a traditional dance performed by Basotho women while kneeling. The dancers move their shoulders and upper bodies to the rhythm of traditional songs.

Mohobelo

Mohobelo is a traditional dance performed by Basotho men. It involves energetic movements, strong steps and singing. It is often performed during cultural celebrations.

Makoloane

Makoloane are initiated Basotho boys who take part in traditional singing and cultural activities. Their songs help to keep Basotho traditions alive.

Learn More About Basotho Traditions

Traditional songs are used during celebrations, cultural events and other important occasions. They help pass stories and traditions from one generation to another.

Basotho traditional dances express culture, unity and history. Examples include Mokhibo and Mohobelo.
