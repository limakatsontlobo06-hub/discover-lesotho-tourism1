Products Found in Lesotho

Mangangajane

These are traditional dried fruits. Basotho make them to preserve the fruits and prevent them from going bad. Mangangajane is a nice snack sold at affordable prices.

Mafi

Mafi is traditional Basotho sour milk. It is made by allowing milk to ferment in a warm place. It is delicious and provides nutrients for the body.

Botle Vaseline

Botle Vaseline is made using aloe vera. It helps moisturise the skin and gives the skin a healthy glow.

Letlotlo Flour

Letlotlo flour is produced in Lesotho. It is used to prepare food and provides energy to the body.
